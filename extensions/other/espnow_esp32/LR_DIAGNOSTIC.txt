ESP-NOW Long Range diagnostic build

Added block:
  ESP-NOWのWi-Fiモード診断をシリアルモニターに表示する

Use it immediately after the ESP-NOW begin block.
The block starts Serial at 115200 baud and prints the protocol bitmap returned by esp_wifi_get_protocol().

Expected examples:
NORMAL: 802.11b/g/n ON, Long Range OFF
LR ONLY: Long Range ON, 802.11b/g/n OFF

This is an experimental build for LR verification.
